Legal / Privacy

Privacy, in plain language.

Vicaro is a self-hosted personal assistant. There is no Vicaro backend server for your chats or pages, and no telemetry. Signing in with Google or an email magic link connects your browsers and devices automatically; the only remote service that sees your pages is the model endpoint you choose.

The short version. Page content and tab metadata are processed on your machine and sent only to the model endpoint you configure. Signing in with Google or an email magic link verifies your identity through Firebase Authentication so your browsers and web UI connect automatically. Vicaro does not sell data, does not run ads, and does not send your pages or chats to us.

What Vicaro is

Vicaro has two parts: a Chrome extension that reads the page you are on and performs the click or keystroke an assistant asks for, and a helper program you install and run yourself. The helper decides what to do, talks to the model you configure, and stores its data locally. They talk over a loopback connection.

Vicaro is closed-source software. The helper is self-hosted, and its local data directory can be deleted by you.

What the extension can see

Only what it needs to carry out a request you make:

  • The list of open tabs, including titles and addresses.
  • The visible content and interactive elements of a tab after you grant page access to that site.
  • The action it is asked to perform, and the page state afterwards.
  • Your signed-in account email and display name (if provided), verified through Firebase Authentication.

Depending on the pages you open, this can include website content, browsing history in the form of open tabs, personal communications such as email or chat pages, and account identifiers. It does not read pages in the background, and it does not run on sites you have not granted. Outside account sign-in below, nothing is sent anywhere except to your own helper and the model endpoint it is configured to use.

Permissions and optional capabilities

The extension asks for page access per site, the first time a task needs it. There is no broad host permission at install. Two capabilities are off until you enable them in Settings, Access:

  • Deep control (debugger) uses Chrome's debugger attachment for trusted input, screenshots, DevTools inspection and file upload. Chrome does not allow this permission to be optional, so it is declared at install and attached only while a task needs it.
  • History and closed tabs lets the assistant search your browsing history and reopen a closed tab, only when you ask it to.

Both can be removed at any time from Chrome's extension settings.

Account sign-in (Google or email magic link)

Signing in is required to connect browsers, the local web UI, and future devices to your helper automatically without manual pairing codes. You can sign in with Google or with a passwordless email magic link through Firebase Authentication. The helper verifies your Firebase ID token against Google's public signing keys and stores your account's Firebase user id, email address, and display name (if provided) locally in account.json. The ID token itself is not stored.

Each enrolled browser receives a local, revocable device credential stored (hashed) in devices.json so reconnects continue working offline and individual devices can be revoked. While signed in, your account name and email are included in the assistant context sent to your model endpoint. Google and Firebase receive only the authentication request (and send the sign-in email when you choose email magic link). No chats, pages, screenshots, or memories are sent to Google or Firebase. Signing out clears the local account link and disconnects enrolled devices on that helper.

Where your data goes

  • Your model endpoint. Messages, page text and tool results are sent to the model server you configure: a local server, your LAN, or a hosted provider. That server's privacy policy applies to it. Vicaro has no default provider and no fallback.
  • Firebase Authentication (Google / email sign-in). Receives only the sign-in request needed to verify your identity (and deliver email sign-in links). No page or chat content is included.
  • Nowhere else. No analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics, no advertising identifiers, and no sale or sharing of data with third parties.

What is stored, and where

  • On your machine: conversation history and task state live in an encrypted SQLite store owned by the helper. Memories and settings live in the same owner-only data directory.
  • In Chrome: the panel stores your helper address, revocable device credential, theme and model preference in chrome.storage.local.
  • Signed-in account and devices: the helper stores your account's name, email and Firebase user id in account.json, and hashed per-device credentials in devices.json. Signing out clears both.
  • Model API keys are stored by the helper and are never returned to the panel.

This website & automatic updates

This site is a static site served by GitHub Pages. Marketing, support and privacy pages do not set cookies, run analytics, or load third-party scripts. The /auth sign-in page loads the Firebase Authentication SDK solely to complete Google or email magic link sign-in and hand your verified token to your local helper. GitHub may keep standard web-server logs under its own privacy policy. The helper installer and release archive are served by GitHub Releases; the extension is distributed by the Chrome Web Store. When installed in Applications, Vicaro Helper checks GitHub Releases periodically for newer versions and verifies each download's SHA-256 checksum before installing (you can pause auto-updates from the menu bar at any time); update checks carry no personal data, page content, or account identifiers.

Your controls

  • Revoke page access or the optional permissions in chrome://extensions, or in Settings, Access.
  • Revoke an individual browser or device from the Vicaro Helper menu bar or the local web UI Connections page.
  • Delete the helper's data directory to erase history, tasks and memories. The menu bar uninstall keeps it on purpose.
  • Sign out in the Vicaro Helper menu bar, the web UI, or Settings, General, Account to disconnect your account.
  • Uninstall the extension and the helper to remove everything.

Children

Vicaro is a developer tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the next release and is dated above. The latest version is always at vicaro.creativeducks.com/privacy.html.
